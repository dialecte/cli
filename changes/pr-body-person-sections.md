### Added

- A brief may hold `sections`, the sections a lot writes for its reviewer (`[{ "heading": "Deviations from the design", "text": "..." }]`): each heading one line without `#`, each text markdown. Every render of the lot's change request (`lot land`, `lot describe`, `lot approve`) shows them verbatim, at the template's `<!-- dialecte:reviewer-sections -->` marker, else just before the kept part, under headings at the level of the template heading above them. `dialecte lot brief` adds, rewrites, reorders or removes them, with who and why.
- The cli's pull request template marks `<!-- dialecte:reviewer-sections -->` after the summary; other packages get it at their next `dialecte ci sync`, and keep rendering the sections before the kept part until then.
- Each send of a change request's text appends the hashes of the lines it sent above the kept heading that the lot's `sent-body.jsonl` lacks, per change request and never rewritten.

### Changed

- **Breaking:** `lot land`, `lot describe` and `lot approve` refuse, sending nothing, a change request body that holds above the kept heading a line the cli never sent there and the new render lacks (a paragraph, a heading, a fenced block written on the host). The refusal names the first three lines and how many there are, and the ways out: record them in the brief's `sections` (or the record they belong to), move them under the kept heading, or remove them from the body. A body no `sent-body.jsonl` covers (sent by an older cli) is checked by its headings at the template's level and its unclosed blocks only, once: the render that passes records it. A task box above the kept heading follows the records, so a box ticked there is re-rendered, never refused. Before, such text was dropped with no word.
- **Breaking:** every send reads the change request again just before the host call and renders over that body, so an edit made while `lot land` pushed is carried (under the kept heading) or refused (above it), never overwritten. A change request no longer open at that second read is refused. A refusal after `lot land`'s push says the head is pushed and the body is not updated (for a change request it just opened, that its lines are not recorded), and running `lot land` again sends it; a refusal after `lot approve` recorded its approval says it is not queued, and running `lot approve` again queues it.
- **Breaking:** a brief's sections are refused at `lot start` and `lot brief` when malformed (not a list of at most 20 `{ heading, text }`, a heading empty, on two lines, led by `#`, over 120 characters or repeated, a text empty, holding a renderer comment line, a task box, a fence, comment or HTML block it never closes, or a `<details>` it never closes, more than 20 000 characters in all) or holding a reference, a plan entry id or a link; a path is checked by the render, against the lot's tree. A render refuses a section heading equal to a template heading, or a text line equal to the line the kept part starts at.
- **Breaking:** the change request template marker pattern takes hyphenated part names, and a template marking a part the renderer does not know is refused.
- `lot describe` resolves its lot as a writing verb (an index two stores share refuses, naming the full lot name), except with `--print`.
- A `sent-body.jsonl` that does not read refuses every render, naming the file and the way out (remove it: the next render checks the body's headings and records again); a line that does not parse (a crash mid-append) is skipped wherever it sits; a line that parses into no row of schema 1 refuses.
