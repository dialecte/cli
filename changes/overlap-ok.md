### Added

- `dialecte lot start <brief> --overlap-ok --by <who> --reason <text>` and `dialecte lot scope <id> --add <path> --overlap-ok --by <who> --reason <text>`: a scope meeting another open lot is accepted instead of refused, for an overlap that names its paths (an other lot whose scope cannot be read, or a damaged one, stays refused, the `lot scope` refusal saying `--overlap-ok` cannot accept it). Each accepted overlap is recorded in the lot folder's `overlaps.jsonl` (the other lot, the met pairs, who and why; `lot scope` writes it before the add, and an add taken back undoes its record), printed by the start and its dry run (`! overlaps <lot>: ...`), and shown on the hand-over card's `scope` line as a warning, as is a line of the file that does not read. The re-check after `lot start` and after `lot scope` refuses a lot the go-ahead did not name, and an accepted lot that widened meanwhile. `--by` and `--reason` of an accepted overlap reach the public records: a path, a link, a reference or an entry id of the brief's plan in either is refused, as are records too long for one append, before anything is written. `lot start --overlap-ok` without `--by` and `--reason`, and `lot scope --overlap-ok` without `--add`, are refused.
