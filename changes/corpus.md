### Added

- `dialecte corpus fetch | list | check | add`: the open-licence corpora (19 sources admitted by the licence review) are fetched by the cli from a manifest it ships, each download pinned by sha256 and each source refused without its licence, terms and verdict. They land in a git-ignored cache (`$DIALECTE_CORPORA_DIR`, else `corpora/` in the cli cache folder), are rebuilt offline from the kept downloads, and are never committed. An archive member outside its folder is refused. `list --json` names every corpus with its state (fetched, stale, missing) and its entry schemas, the personal licensed entries of `config.yml` `corpora:` included; `check` exits 1 on a cached file that no longer matches its fetch; `add` writes a licensed entry, keeping the file's comments.
