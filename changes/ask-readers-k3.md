### Added

- `dialecte ask pack <ask-id> [--out <dir>] [--outside] [--web] [--lot <id|folder>] [--json]`: opens the ask's next round of readers. The pool is rebuilt from what the ask recorded (`request.json` beside `ask.json`: the request and the manifest of the documents read, each file's sha256 and lines; a file moved, removed or added since refuses, exit 2, naming the ask to make again): the ask's list first, then a wider search. Reader A reads by rank, B by angle family, C (effort high) as B without the top slice, each up to 1,500 non-noise lines; a later round gives no reader a slice its letter had, and withholds a slice found only by an angle a verified cite already came from. `rounds/round-<r>/` gets `reader-<A|B|C>.md` and `.json` (the slices with their original line numbers, the report schema, a nonce each) and `pack.json` last (`schema: 1`: each reader's letter, nonce, slice ids and the slices as the ranges it was given, `{ id, file, from, to, clause, linked }`); packing an open round again prints the same briefs, nothing rewritten; `--out` copies them. `--outside` opens the one round of outside readers of an ask without documents (no slice; `--web` lets them search the web). After a high round closed at `needs-adjudicator`, the pack writes the adjudicator's brief instead (`adjudicator.md` and `.json`: the accepted reports with their checks, the union of their cited ranges, a nonce). Exit 0 the briefs ready; exit 1 nothing new to give: the round is recorded skipped (`rounds/round-<r>/skipped.json`, `schema: 1`, `{ ask, round, state: "needs-person", reason, at }`, written once), the ask is at `needs-person` and the next command named (a high documents round with an answering accepted report goes to the adjudicator's brief instead); exit 2 usage, an unknown or answered ask, an ask without `request.json`, a changed file, `--outside` on a documents ask or the reverse.
- `dialecte ask verdict <ask-id> [<report.json>...] [--missing <letter>]... [--adjudicator] [--lot <id|folder>] [--json]`: checks the reports of the open round and closes it. A report is accepted against the ask, the round, the nonce issued to its reader and not used (claimed by an exclusive file under `rounds/round-<r>/used/`), one report per session and no body equal to an earlier one across every round of the ask; each cite must name a file of the manifest unchanged, lie inside a slice given to its reader (the ranges `pack.json` recorded), span at most 40 non-noise lines, and quote 8 to 40 words (or one whole table row or code line) found on word boundaries in those lines only; each claim must name a key term and the subject and back the verdict; a claim saying the opposite of the question is a polarity conflict that never counts toward agreement; a report may name its model `tier`. Every reader the round gave a brief needs a report file, or `--missing <letter>` when it wrote nothing (counted refused `missing`; with every letter declared, no file is needed); else exit 2 naming `--missing <letter>`, nothing recorded. Reports agree on the verdict, the value (as sets for a list) and a shared line or clause: effort low 2 readers 1 round, medium 2 readers 2 rounds, high 3 readers 3 rounds, a verified dissent asking for another round; nothing verified searches again while something is left, up to 5 rounds; a round with nothing left goes to the adjudicator at high (`--adjudicator`, its one report checked against its brief's ranges: verified, the fact at rung 5) or to the person. Outside readers answer in one round: every reader agreeing, none not-known or hedged, no polarity conflict and not contradicting the definition read live (`contradicts-definition`), recorded `unchecked` by source (`model`, `web`). A re-ask whose readers agree with another answer than its unchecked fact (or a person's fact without `--verdict`) records nothing: `needs-person`, reason `reask-differs`, the readers' answer kept. An agreed answer is recorded as a fact (the union of the verified cites, the shortest answer, `by.tiers` the agreeing readers' tiers). The round keeps `verdict.json` (`schema: 1`: each report with its claims' checks, a claim reading a poor block flagged `quality: "poor"`, an accepted report's session, body sha256, answer, `familiesRead`, `notFound`, `notKnown`; the outcome and reason, the angles verified, the state, next and fact), `accepted.json` for the adjudicator, and `answering.json` while it closes (`schema: 1`: the nonces claimed and the fact draft's key), so a retry after a crash finishes the same round on the fact already recorded. Exit 0 answered, 1 the person next (in a store lot the question raised once, after the round is closed, its details pointing to the round's verdict; next `dialecte lot answer <lot> <question>`, else `dialecte ask answer <id> "<answer>" --by <who>`), 2 usage or a refusal (nothing recorded: a round closed, in progress, no report of the round), 3 another round or the adjudicator next (`dialecte ask pack <id>`). `--json` keys: `schema`, `ask`, `round`, `state`, `reason` (null once answered), `reports` (never a session, body hash or reader text), `fact`, `conflict`, `next`. Events: `ask.state`, `ask.verdict` (`{ round, state, reason, accepted, refused: { <reason>: n }, polarityConflicts, adjudicator }`), `ask.fact` on a recorded fact (`{ hit: null, candidates: 0, new, stale: 0, rung }`), `ask.pack` (`{ round, readers, skipped }`); never a question, answer, quote or url.
- `dialecte ask answer <ask-id> "<answer>" --by <who> [--verdict yes|no|depends|value] [--cite <file id>:<from>-<to> --quote "<quote>"] [--lot <id|folder>] [--json]`: the person answers an ask that is not answered (rung 6). A person fact by `<who>`, `unchecked: true` when the ask read no documents or went `--person-only`; `answer.json` in the ask folder (`schema: 1`). On an ask the outside readers answered, the person confirms it: a person fact supersedes the outside fact (it stays `unchecked: true` while the ask read no documents, so it is asked again once documents exist) and `<ask>/confirm.json` (`schema: 1`, `{ ask, fact, by, at }`) marks the ask confirmed; a second confirmation is refused (exit 2, naming it confirmed); every other answered ask stays refused. `--verdict` keeps that verdict on the person fact (`value`: the answer as its value), so a later re-ask whose readers agree with it supersedes it; without it the fact says `person` and a re-ask's readers are shown to the person; another word is refused (exit 2, naming it and the four). `--cite` (with `--quote`) is checked as a reader cite against the whole documents and recorded as a reader miss (given or not to a reader, and the first round) or `citeRefused` (a cite into a file changed since the ask: `file-changed`), the answer recorded all the same. `--json` (`schema: 1`): `ask`, `fact`, `miss`, `citeRefused`. Exit 0 recorded, 2 usage or a refusal (an unknown or answered ask, one made before the readers, another run holding the ask). Events `ask.answer` (`{ rung: 6, miss, citeRefused }`) and `ask.state`. `lot answer` on the question an ask raised records the same person fact through a registered `onAnswered` hook (synchronous, the lot note written before it returns).
- `ask verdict` and `ask answer` hold `<ask>/ask.claim`, so one of them records the ask's fact and the other is refused as in progress (exit 2); a verdict also holds `rounds/round-<r>/verdict.claim`. A claim file holds its holder's pid and start time; a pid running under another start time (`ps` run in the C locale) is a pid reused, a claim of a gone process is taken over once older than its stale limit, under an exclusive `<claim>.takeover` file, and any claim held more than 10 minutes is taken over.
- The lot's `standard.json` gains the state `outside`, written by `ask` when any ask of the lot past rung 2 went outside the documents (the note lists the documents asks beside them), with `confirmed: true|false` (a person answered or confirmed every outside ask). The card's standard line warns for it at every effort and fails at effort high unless `confirmed` is true: the flag decides, never the note text.
- `dialecte lot standard <id|folder> outside <note>`: records the `outside` state by hand, `confirmed: false`.
- `dialecte standard eval <gold.jsonl> --packs [--effort low|medium|high]`: the recall at 5 and 10 of the slices the round-1 readers are given (reader A in its order, and A and B together, a slice met twice kept once; `packs` in `--json`). `--reports <asks folder>`: the agreed-wrong rate of recorded asks whose question equals a gold question, judged by their last readers' round (`reports: { agreed, wrong, unjudged, rate }` in `--json`, `rate: null` when nothing is judged). A gold line may hold `kind` (`fact`, `polarity`, `exception`, `not-in-text`), `answer` and `status`.

### Changed

- `dialecte ask`: `ask pack`, `ask verdict` and `ask answer` hand the ask on (no longer refused as not built yet); in a store lot a `needs-person` ask raises one question on the lot and its next is `dialecte lot answer <lot> <question>`.
